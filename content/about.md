I'm **Pin Xiong**. **Apache Dubbo Committer, AWS Community Builder, and speaker.**

10+ years in the internet industry: microservices, middleware, cloud native, and Serverless. These days I spend most of my time on one narrow question, because it is the question my day job keeps forcing: **what actually changes when AI writes code inside a production system?**

Not the demo. The system that is already live, has no documentation, and cannot be allowed to fail.

## What I work on

- **AI-assisted engineering.** Multi-agent refactoring flows with separated producer and reviewer roles, so the model that writes the change never grades its own work.
- **Microservices and middleware.** Long enough in the Dubbo ecosystem to have opinions about what breaks at scale, and to have committed the fixes upstream.
- **Cloud native and Serverless on AWS.** Multi-region deployments, IAM least privilege, and the operational cost of getting it wrong.
